For foreigners who are attending our event with clear and specific intent such as:

- Speakers
- Staff from our Sponsors
- Community booth staff
- Organizers and volunteers

and cannot apply for other C-3 series visas (such as C-3-9 or other) by themselves. We may provide a visa invitation letter that you can use to apply for a C-3-1 visa.

To request a visa invitation letter, please click the link below and fill out the form.

[Request Visa Invitation Letter](https://app.formbricks.com/s/cmtp29v25tjao01vfx10t82lh)

Please note that K-ETA isn't considered as a types of visa and the link to the form above is only for requesting a visa invitation letter for C-3-1 visa applications.
