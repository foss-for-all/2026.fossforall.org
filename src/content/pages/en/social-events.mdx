In addition to the conference program, FOSS for All Conference 2026 offers a variety of social events to help participants get to know one another better. Please note that these events are limited to eligible participants, who will receive separate invitations from the organizing committee!

## Eve Festival

Join us the evening before the conference to chat and get to know fellow participants.

- Date and time: Friday, November 27, 2026, at 8:00 PM
- Venue: Namdo Hof & Namdo Nakji (남도호프&남도낙지, 1F, 19 Eonju-ro 71-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul)
- Participation fee: KRW 20,000
- Naver Maps: https://naver.me/5apH7r0W
- Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/Vc21UHGrTyy5Jrru7
- OpenStreetMap: https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=37.496996&mlon=127.043444#map=18/37.496996/127.043444

## After Party

After the conference program ends, join us to celebrate a successful event and network with fellow conference contributors.

- Date and time: Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 7:00 PM (doors open at 6:45 PM)
- Venue: Hive Yeoksam Nouvella Hall (하이브 역삼 누벨라홀, B1 #101, 17 Eonju-ro 71-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul / Geumgang Building, 1F Cleantopia & Woori Hardware)
- Eligible participants: Organizing committee members, volunteers, speakers, poster presenters, individual supporters, sponsor representatives, and community booth representatives
  - Please register in advance using the registration link emailed to eligible participants!
- Naver Maps: https://naver.me/G5PB1HuT
- Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=17+Eonju-ro+71-gil,+Gangnam-gu,+Seoul

## Day Trip

On the day after the conference, spend the day with international participants exploring Suwon Hwaseong Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

- Date and time: Sunday, November 29, 2026, 09:00 - 19:00 (approximate; subject to on-site conditions and traffic)
- Meeting and return point: Exit 9 of Yangjae Station (in front of Seocho Cultural & Arts Center, Seocho-gu, Seoul), **meet at 09:00** (chartered bus departs around 09:10 - 09:20)
- Participation fee: Announced separately via the pre-registration invitation email
- Eligible participants: Organizing committee members, volunteers, international participants (speakers and community), and microgrant project participants (first-come, first-served)
  - Seating is limited; please register in advance using the registration link provided!
- Itinerary (times are approximate and subject to change):
  - 09:00 Meet at Yangjae Station Exit 9 (depart around 09:10 - 09:20)
  - 10:00 - 10:50 Suwon Hwaseong Museum
  - 11:00 - 11:20 Muye24gi traditional martial arts performance (Sinpungnu Plaza)
  - 11:30 - 12:30 Korean lunch
  - 12:30 - 13:20 Hwaseong Haenggung Palace
  - 13:30 - 14:30 English guided fortress wall walk (Janganmun -> Hwahongmun -> Banghwasuryujeong -> Yeonmudae)
  - 15:00 - 15:50 Traditional Korean archery at Yeonmudae
  - 16:00 - 17:20 Traditional tea at Cafe Dohwa Yeonmudae
  - 17:30 Depart Suwon -> 18:30 - 19:00 Estimated arrival at Yangjae Station Exit 9 (subject to traffic)
- What to wear: Warm coat, comfortable walking shoes, and gloves are recommended for outdoor walking.
- Naver Maps (meeting point): https://naver.me/5kxjMNXu
- Google Maps (meeting point): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Seocho+Cultural+and+Arts+Center,+Yangjae+Station,+Seoul
