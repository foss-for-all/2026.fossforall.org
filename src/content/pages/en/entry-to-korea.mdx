## Immigration
To enter Korea, you will need to bring
- Valid passport or other travel document
- If you are eligible for visa waiver, K-ETA. If not, a visa is required.
- Arrival card: Either paper one or [E-Arrival card](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr)

### Getting your Visa or K-ETA
- Check if you need to bring a visa using [TravelDoc](https://www.traveldoc.aero/) or [Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/).
- If you are eligible for visa waiver, and need to apply for K-ETA, visit the [K-ETA website](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/) to apply for it.
- Please refer to [Request Visa Invitation Letter](../visa-letter-request/) in case you need our visa invitation letter to apply for C-3-1 visa.

## Quarantine

If you are entering from or have traveled to one of the Quarantine Inspection Required Areas, you must either enter your information in Q-Code in advance or fill out and submit a Health Questionnaire.

You can enter your Q-Code information starting seven days prior to your arrival to Korea. If you complete your Q-Code registration before you board the flight, you can skip the Health Questionnaire and just show your Q-Code QR code instead during the quarantine screening. 

[Link to Q-Code website](https://qcode.kdca.go.kr)

## Customs
1. All passengers entering Korea with items to declare are required to fill out a customs declaration form. While all individual travelers must fill out a form, only one form per family is required when traveling with family members. Travelers who have a postal shipment must fill out two forms to submit one upon customs clearance during baggage claim and the other to the airline staff, who will clear customs upon arrival of the shipment on behalf of the traveler. Please contact your airline for details in regards to receiving postal shipment.
2. Passengers entering Korea through Incheon International Airport Terminal 2 or Gimpo International Airport can submit their customs declaration form on the mobile app or website starting August 1, 2022.

Tax Exemption Regulation per Person
- Each traveler is limited to purchases valued up to USD 800 in total
- Alcohol: Limited up to the value of USD 400, 2 bottles, 2 liters in total per person
- Cigarettes: Limited to 200 cigarettes; no price value limit
- Perfume: Limited to 100 ml; no price value limit

Note: Travelers ages 19 and younger cannot receive tax exemption for alcohol or cigarettes

You may either fill out the paper form or use the [official website](https://m.customs.go.kr/tms/html/mos/psnr/MOS4001001Q.do)
