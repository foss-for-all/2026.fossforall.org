Thank you for confirming your participation as a sponsor of FOSS for All Conference 2026! This guide provides the information you need to participate in the event after completing the sponsorship agreement process.

## When Sponsorship Benefits are provided

As a general rule, sponsorship benefits begin once payment of the sponsorship fee has been confirmed. However, when sponsorship is confirmed late, payment may be made just one or two weeks before the event, or even after it. In these cases, the following criteria apply:

- If a written agreement is used: Benefits begin once both parties have signed or stamped the agreement.
- If no written agreement is used: Benefits begin when FOSS for All issues a tax invoice (for sponsors transacting as Korean businesses) or an invoice (for sponsors transacting as overseas businesses).

## Registration for Sponsor Representatives

Sponsor representatives are not registered automatically. Each person attending must register by themselves individually. Please follow the steps below, and the organizers will approve your registration.

- Visit the [registration page](https://ticketa.co/event/ytbqha4p).
- Select [`관계자 및 초청 등록 Staff and Invited registration`](https://ticketa.co/event/ytbqha4p/tickets?phase=346).
- Select your ticket type:
  - If you are staffing a sponsor booth: Select `후원사 부스 담당자 Sponsor booth staff`.
  - If you are using a complimentary ticket included in your sponsorship benefits: Select `<Sponsor Name> Complimentary Tickets` (or `<후원사 이름> 증정 티켓`) for your organization.
  - If you are presenting a sponsor session: Select `발표자 Speakers`.
- Complete and submit the registration form.
- Ask your organization's sponsorship contact to request approval from the organizers. We will review and approve the registration.

Sponsor representatives must also register within the registration period stated on the registration page. Due to the venue's access policy, it is difficult to accommodate exceptional registrations after the deadline. Please ensure that everyone registers on time.

If any of your representatives are foreign nationals traveling from abroad and need a visa invitation letter from the organizers, please refer to [Request Visa Invitation Letter](../visa-letter-request/) for instructions.

## Submitting Logos and Promotional Videos

- Sponsor logos
  - If you submitted your logo through the sponsor registration form, you do not need to submit it again. If your logo has changed or you were unable to submit it through the form, please email it to the sponsorship team.
  - Please submit your logo as an SVG vector file whenever possible. If this is not possible, please provide a sufficiently high-resolution PNG file with a transparent background.
  - If you have not yet submitted your logo, or need to update it, please submit it no later than three weeks before the event. This deadline allows us to meet the printing schedule for promotional materials featuring sponsor logos.
- Sponsor promotional videos
  - Gold sponsors may provide a promotional video to be played for attendees during breaks.
  - Videos must have a 16:9 aspect ratio, a resolution of at least 1080p, and a maximum duration of three minutes.
  - If you would like to use this benefit, please submit your video no later than seven days before the event.

## Attendee List

Gold and Silver sponsors receive an attendee list as part of their sponsorship benefits. We plan to provide it as a spreadsheet approximately one week after the event. The file will include only attendees who have consented to sharing their information with third parties. You may use and retain this data until November 28, 2026.
