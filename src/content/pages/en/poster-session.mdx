A poster session will take place during lunch at this event. In a poster session, presenters display large-format posters, such as A0-sized posters, introducing their projects, discuss them with attendees, and answer questions. At this FOSS for All Conference, each organization awarded a FOSS for All Microgrants 2026 grant will present a poster and be available for discussion and Q&A. Stop by Room 102 (Builders Room) during lunch to join in!

[Meet the FOSS for All Microgrants 2026 recipients](https://fossforall.org/docs/microgrants/2026/)
