This guide is intended for visitors arriving from overseas.

## From Incheon International Airport
### AREX Express Train
The AREX Express Train travels directly from the airport to Seoul Station. You can buy tickets online or at the station.

This is a good option if you are staying near Seoul Station, Myeongdong, or Jongno.

### AREX All Stop Train
This is a good option if you are staying near the venue. It stops at every station, so you can transfer at Hongik University Station (Line 2) or Gimpo Airport Station (Line 9).

Like the rest of the Seoul subway, the All Stop Train does not accept direct contactless credit card payments (open-loop payments). Buy a single-journey ticket with cash or a card before boarding.

### Airport Bus
You can buy a ticket on the arrivals floor. If you have a Korean transportation card (other than the Climate Card), you can also board without buying a separate ticket. Fares are approximately KRW 15,000–20,000.

The following airport buses serve the area near the venue:

* Regular: 6009, 6020, 6703
* Late night (23:20–04:30): N6703

### Taxi
You can use the Uber app to book a taxi in Korea, or use the taxi stand outside the airport arrivals floor.

Depending on traffic, a taxi from Incheon International Airport to the area near the venue generally costs KRW 50,000–70,000. A late-night surcharge of 20–40% applies.
If you are charged an excessive fare in the hundreds of thousands of won, use the Seoul Metropolitan Government’s [reporting page](https://cartax.seoul.go.kr:21334/interview/bvics/bvicsInfo/selectLanguage.do).

For more information, visit the [Incheon International Airport website](https://www.airport.kr/ap_en/1514/subview.do).

## From Gimpo International Airport
### AREX
The AREX Express Train does not stop at Gimpo International Airport; only the All Stop Train is available. See the AREX All Stop Train section above for details.

### Subway
Subway Line 9 can take you to the area near the venue without a transfer. See below for details on using the subway.

### Taxi
You can use the Uber app to book a taxi in Korea, or use the taxi stand outside the airport arrivals floor.

Depending on traffic, a taxi from Gimpo International Airport to the area near the venue generally costs KRW 25,000–40,000. A late-night surcharge of 20–40% applies.
If you are charged an excessive fare in the hundreds of thousands of won, use the Seoul Metropolitan Government’s [reporting page](https://cartax.seoul.go.kr:21334/interview/bvics/bvicsInfo/selectLanguage.do).

For more information, visit the [Gimpo International Airport website](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimpoeng/cms/frCon/index.do?MENU_ID=270&CONTENTS_NO=4).

* Cash

You can buy a single-journey ticket with cash at a machine in each subway station.

After your journey, insert the ticket into a deposit refund machine near the fare gates to recover the KRW 500 deposit.

* Short-term Climate Card

This transportation card provides unlimited travel on eligible subways and buses in Seoul for a fixed period. You can buy it with cash or a credit card from **newer** transportation card vending machines in stations on the **underground section** of Line 1 and on Lines 2–8. It is also sold at some convenience stores.

The card cannot be used on GTX-A or the Shinbundang Line, even for journeys within Seoul. For more details, visit the [Seoul Metropolitan Government website](https://english.seoul.go.kr/policy/transportation/climate-card/).

* Transportation card

Regular transportation cards can also be purchased with cash at **newer** vending machines in subway stations or at convenience stores.

Cards can be reloaded with cash only at subway station machines and convenience stores. They cannot be reloaded on buses.

Board through the front door and exit through the rear door. Tap your transportation card when boarding and **again when getting off**.

Many bus routes do not accept cash. If you plan to take a bus, obtain a short-term Climate Card or a transportation card as described above.

* Climate Card accepted: Seoul-operated buses (excluding metropolitan buses; see the image), and neighborhood buses
* Climate Card not accepted: airport buses, metropolitan buses (red buses), and buses operated by municipalities outside Seoul

## Returning to the Airport
You can return using the same transportation options you used to travel from the airport to Seoul.

If you have a Climate Card, you can take AREX to Gimpo International Airport or Incheon International Airport at no additional charge (the reverse journey is not covered).
