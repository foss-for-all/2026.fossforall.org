Thank you for participating as a booth operator at the FOSS for All Conference 2026! This guide provides essential information for booth managers, including schedules for setup and teardown, available equipment, and relevant policies.

## Shipping Materials for Your Booth
You may send materials for your booth in advance to be stored on-site before the event. Please refer to the link below for further details:

https://forum.fossforall.org/t/foss-for-all-conference-2026-logistics-contact-person-info/193?u=ybhan

## Booth Setup and Teardown Schedule

- Setup Hours
  - Friday, November 27 (the day before the event): 18:30 – 20:00
  - Saturday, November 28 (the day of the event): From 08:30
- Teardown
  - Begins at 17:00 on the day of the event.
  - It is recommended to complete teardown by 17:30; all items must be cleared by 18:30 at the latest.

## Booth Setup Details

- Each booth includes:
  - One table (approx. 1.7m wide)
  - Two chairs
  - One power strip
- Please note that tablecloths, X-banners, and backdrops are not provided. You are welcome to bring your own, provided they do not obstruct the flow of attendee traffic.

## QR Code Scanner for Booth Visitors

- A web-based QR code scanner will be provided for booth managers to use. 
- You can access the web app and scan the QR codes on participants' name tags to collect information about those who visit your booth.
- The collected data will be compiled and sent to you within two business days after the event concludes.

## Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding the event, please feel free to contact the organizing committee at any time.

Email address: `conference at fossforall.org`
